Finance Hub – Privacy Policy

Finance Hub is a personal tool used only by its owner, for the owner's own Gmail account.

What it accesses

It uses the read-only Gmail permission (gmail.readonly) to read emails under a single label and download their bill and receipt attachments. It cannot send, delete, or change any email.

How data is used and stored

Downloaded files and a small record of which emails were processed are stored only on the owner's own computer and personal cloud drive, for personal bookkeeping. Nothing is sent to any other server.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any AI or machine learning models.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
